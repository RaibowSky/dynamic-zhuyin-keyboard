libtabe notices
===============

McBopomofo's BPMFMappings.txt, used by tools/merge_mcbopomofo_dictionary.py to add
phrase readings to zhuyin_cedict.tsv, was originally simplified from tsi.src of
libtabe and subsequently modified (see McBopomofo Source/Data/README.md at commit
14f672cd9296deb4ff87034b05003b15a1e796f5).

The exact libtabe release behind tsi.src is not recorded upstream. libtabe 0.2.5
carried a four-clause BSD-style license with an advertising acknowledgement;
libtabe 0.2.6 carries a three-clause version without it. Both are reproduced
below. The acknowledgements are displayed here, in the app's open-source
licenses page and in NOTICE. If the 0.2.5 terms apply, any advertising material
that mentions features or use of this data must display them as well:

  This product includes software developed by
  Pai-Hsiang Hsiao and contributors for TaBE Project.

  This product includes software developed by the
  Computer Systems and Communication Lab,
  Institute of Information Science, Academia Sinica
  and its contributors.

Debian's libtabe record also lists:
  Copyright 1996 Chih-Hao Tsai @ Beckman Institute, University of Illinois

Sources:
  https://sources.debian.org/src/libtabe/0.2.6-1.2/COPYING/
  https://sources.debian.org/src/libtabe/0.2.6-1.2/debian/copyright/
  (debian/copyright reproduces the libtabe 0.2.5 COPYING file)
